# UtterInk identity handoff

## Selected direction

Use **Ink Caret Monogram / B · Right Cursor** as the identity foundation. The selected menu-bar silhouette is an open U with the right stem interrupted to form a text cursor.

## Preserve

- Exact spelling: `UtterInk`.
- Quiet, native, privacy-first, crafted tone.
- Open U bowl and deliberate right-side cursor gap.
- One-color transparent template artwork at 16–20pt.
- Shared geometry across menu bar, App Icon, wordmark companion, and state icons.

## Avoid

- Centered power-button geometry.
- Microphones, waveforms, speech bubbles, droplets, quills, AI sparkles, or gradients.
- Raster cutouts and white matte edges.
- Color-only status communication.
- Fine folds or counters that disappear at menu-bar size.

## Next production step

Pixel-fit the selected vector at 16, 18, and 20pt. Derive a restrained color App Icon, deterministic wordmark, and recording/processing/success/failure template family. Validate light/dark appearance, high contrast, competitor similarity, and trademark risk before shipping.
